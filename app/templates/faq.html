Frequently Asked Questions

How long do the shortened links last?
Our links are permanent by default. However, you can set custom expiration dates via the advanced settings dashboard if you need temporary redirection for seasonal campaigns.
Are the generated QR codes high-resolution?
Yes. All QR codes are generated as vector-compatible PNGs or SVGs, ensuring they remain crisp when printed on everything from business cards to large-scale billboards.
Can I track the performance of my links?
Absolutely. Every shortened link includes real-time analytics tracking, providing data on click counts, geographic location, and referring devices.
Is there a limit to how many links I can shorten?
Free users can shorten up to 100 links per month. For higher volume requirements and API access, please refer to our developer documentation or enterprise plans.
